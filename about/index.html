About FileKit

Simple file tools that respect your privacy.

On this page
  • Why FileKit exists
  • Who runs it
  • What we promise
  • How the site is paid for
  • Feedback

FileKit is a free set of everyday file tools. You can convert Word, PDF, Excel and images, merge and split PDFs, resize pictures, change photo backgrounds and view documents, all without installing software or creating an account.

Why FileKit exists

Most online converters ask you to upload your file to a server you know nothing about. Contracts, CVs, ID photos and invoices are personal, so FileKit does the work inside your own browser. Your files are not sent to us.

Who runs it

FileKit is built and maintained by Ahsaan, an independent developer. It is a small, self-run project, not a large company. You can reach the owner directly through the Contact page.

What we promise

  • No sign-up and no software to install.
  • Honest tools: every page explains what the tool can and cannot do.
  • Practical guides that explain how to get a good result.
  • Clear privacy rules that you can read on the Privacy Policy page.

How the site is paid for

FileKit is free to use. To cover costs, the site may show advertising, including Google AdSense. Ads never change how the tools work and never receive your files.

Feedback

Found a bug or want a new tool? Please tell us. Real feedback is how the tools improve.
