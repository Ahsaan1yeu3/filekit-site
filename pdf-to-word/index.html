Frequently asked questions

Will the layout stay exactly the same?

No. Text and paragraphs are kept, but exact page layout, columns and fancy fonts may change.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Scanned pages have no real text, so they are added as images instead of editable text.

Does it support Urdu text?

Yes, Urdu and Arabic text is detected and set right-to-left.
