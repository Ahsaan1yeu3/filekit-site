Frequently asked questions

Does resizing reduce quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Enlarging it can make it look blurry.

Can I keep the proportions?

Yes. Keep proportions is on by default so the picture is not stretched.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Resizing happens on your device.
