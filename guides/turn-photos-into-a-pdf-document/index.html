How to turn photos into a PDF document

Phone cameras are the quickest scanners most people own. Assignments, receipts, forms and signed papers often start as photos, but many websites only accept PDF. Converting images to PDF is easy, and a few habits at the photo stage make the result much better.

Take better photos

  • Place the paper on a flat, plain surface in good light.
  • Hold the phone directly above the page so the edges are straight.
  • Avoid shadows from your hand or phone across the text.
  • Check that every word is sharp before moving on.

Prepare the images

Crop away the table or background, and rotate pictures that are sideways. Name them in the order you want, for example page-1, page-2. Large photos from newer phones can be several megabytes each, so ten photos may produce a very large PDF. Use the Resize Image tool first if a website limits the file size.

Create the PDF

  • Open the JPG to PDF tool and choose your images. JPG, PNG and WEBP work.
  • Check the numbered list and use Up and Down to fix the order.
  • Press Create PDF and download images.pdf.

How pages are laid out

Each image goes on its own A4 page and is scaled to fit with a small margin, without stretching. Wide pictures get a landscape page, tall ones get a portrait page. Because the whole picture is kept, a photo with a lot of background will show that background, so crop first for a clean look.

Reduce the file size

If the PDF is too large to upload, resize each photo to about 1600 pixels on the long side and save it as JPG at 80 to 90 percent quality. Text stays readable and the file becomes much smaller.

Be careful with sensitive papers

The tool runs on your device and does not upload your images. Even so, identity cards, bank papers and medical documents should be shared only with people who need them, and deleted from shared folders afterwards.

Final check

Open the finished PDF and scroll through it. Confirm the page order, that nothing is cut off and that text is readable when zoomed. Then give the file a clear name before you send it.
