How to change a photo background for ID and profile pictures

Application forms, job portals and ID systems often ask for a photo on a plain white or coloured background. You can fix a photo taken at home instead of paying for a new one.

Start with a good photo

The best results come from a picture taken in front of a plain wall or sheet, in even light. Avoid shadows on the wall, patterns, and backgrounds that are close in colour to your clothes or hair.

Step by step

  • Upload the photo and click on the background to choose the colour to remove.
  • Adjust the colour match range. A higher value removes more of the background, a lower value protects the subject.
  • Use a small edge softness so the outline does not look rough.
  • Choose a new colour, an image, or a transparent background, then download the PNG.

Fix common problems

If part of your face or clothing disappears, lower the range or click a different spot on the background. If patches of the old background remain, raise the range. Turn off the connected option only if you need to remove background areas that do not touch the picture’s edges.

Check the official requirements

Passports, visas and exams each have exact rules for size, background colour and the amount of head visible. Read the requirements for your application first, then resize the image to the required pixels with the resize tool. Some offices do not accept edited photos, so confirm this before you submit.
