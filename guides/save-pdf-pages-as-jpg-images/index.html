How to save PDF pages as JPG images

Sometimes you need a PDF page as a picture: a slide for a presentation, a flyer for a chat group, or a preview image for a website. Saving pages as JPG images is quick, but it helps to understand what you gain and what you give up.

When images are the right choice

  • Sharing one page in a messaging app that does not preview PDFs well.
  • Adding a page to a slide deck or a design.
  • Posting a flyer or certificate on social media.
  • Making a thumbnail of a document.

How to convert

  • Open the PDF to JPG tool and choose your file.
  • Wait while each page is converted. A progress message shows the current page.
  • Look at the previews and press the Download button under each page you need.

Quality and size

Pages are drawn at twice their normal size, which keeps small text sharp on most screens. Higher quality means a bigger file. If a page is too large for an upload limit, open the JPG in the Resize Image tool and reduce its width or quality.

Limits to know about

  • Up to 60 pages are converted at once. For a longer document, split it first with the Split PDF tool.
  • Each page becomes a separate picture, so you download them one by one.
  • Text in a JPG is part of the picture. You cannot select, search or copy it.

Keep the PDF when text matters

A picture of a page is not the same as the document. If people must search, copy text or use links, share the PDF instead. Use images only for viewing and sharing.

Respect copyright and privacy

Convert only documents you own or have permission to use. If a page contains private details, remember that anyone with the image can see them, so crop or choose different pages before sharing.

Quick checklist

Choose the right file, convert, check each preview for cut-off text or blank pages, download only what you need and name each image with its page number so it stays organised.
