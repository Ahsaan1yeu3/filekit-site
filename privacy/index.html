This policy explains what information FileKit collects, how it is used, and how Google and other third parties may serve ads on this site. By using the site you agree to it.

Your files

Conversions, merging, splitting, resizing, editing and viewing happen locally in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not store, read or share them. Closing the page removes them from your browser memory.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or give personal details. Like most websites, our hosting provider and analytics or advertising partners may automatically receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, language, the pages you visit, and the date and time of your visit. This is used to run the site securely, measure performance and show ads.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. FileKit itself does not need cookies to run its tools. Third-party services, described below, may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features of third-party content may stop working.

Advertising and Google AdSense

FileKit may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at Google Partner Sites.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you may be shown a consent message before cookies are used for personalised ads.

Third-party services

To work, the site loads fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. These providers can see your IP address when your browser requests those files, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, optional phone number and message you type are sent through the FormSubmit service to our inbox and are used only to reply to you.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it. Because we do not collect accounts or personal profiles, there is usually little to share, but you can ask through the Contact page. We do not sell personal information.

Children

FileKit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top of the page when we do. Questions can be sent through the Contact page.
